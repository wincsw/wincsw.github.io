About me

I'm also open to research in sonic/auditory interaction, physical/tangible interaction and fabrication, also design research with speculative approaches. (see more in Research)


My design work (out side of research) scatters around physical computing (Arduino, 3D printing...), sonic interaction, humam-AI collaboration, and immersive experience (see more in Portfolios).
