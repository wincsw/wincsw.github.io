Thesis

Master Thesis - Noise by Choice: AR-Mediated Selective Noise Control for Office Environment


Supervisors: Andrii Matviienko, Abdallah El Ali (CWI, Netherlands), Julian Rasch (LMU, Germany)
Full Thesis

  • Explored the design space and real-world perceived usage of visual interfaces for AR-mediated selective noise control in office context
  • Developed of a VR-simulated-AR prototype and conducted a mixed-method within-subjects study to evaluate interface designs using spatial vs semantic interaction principles
  • Investigated through interview and thematic analysis on how workers perceive the possible practical, social, and ethical benefits and implications of selective noise control system use in workplace
